# Agency Booking Policies — DEMONSTRATION ONLY

These are fictional policies for testing the travel guide. They are not actual contractual terms, legal advice, or a real booking service.
They apply only to the sample package codes CBE-DEMO-02, BLR-DEMO-03, and BLR-DEMO-02.

## Booking procedure

For this demonstration, the traveller requests a package code, preferred date, number of adults, and ages of children. The agency must confirm availability and provide a written quote before collecting payment.
No departure dates, live inventory, payment links, phone numbers, or email addresses are supplied in these sample documents.
The travel guide answers questions only. It cannot reserve rooms, collect payments, or confirm a booking.

## Payment schedule

A fictional deposit of 30% of the quoted package total is due after written confirmation.
The remaining 70% is due 7 calendar days before departure.
For a booking made fewer than 7 calendar days before departure, the full quoted amount is due after written confirmation.

## Example deposit

For a confirmed demonstration quote of INR 24,000, the 30% deposit would be INR 7,200 and the remaining balance would be INR 16,800.

## Customer cancellation charges

Cancellation charges are calculated as a percentage of the confirmed package total, not merely the deposit paid.

| Notice before departure | Cancellation charge |
| --- | --- |
| 15 or more calendar days | 10% of the package total |
| 7–14 calendar days | 30% of the package total |
| 3–6 calendar days | 60% of the package total |
| 0–2 calendar days, or no-show | 100% of the package total |

For example, cancelling a fully paid INR 24,000 package 10 calendar days before departure incurs a fictional INR 7,200 charge and leaves INR 16,800 eligible for refund.
Refund handling when the cancellation charge exceeds the amount already paid is not specified here; ask the agency.

## Refund timing

Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days after the agency confirms the cancellation and refund amount. Bank processing time is additional and is not specified.

## Rescheduling

One date-change request may be submitted at least 10 calendar days before departure.
Approval is subject to availability. Any difference in the package price must be paid by the traveller.
The administrative date-change fee is INR 500 per booking.
Requests made fewer than 10 calendar days before departure require agency review; approval and charges are not specified.

## Meals, transport, and special requests

Only meals explicitly listed in a package are included. Vegetarian meal requests may be submitted, but availability must be confirmed.
Shared transport means the vehicle may carry other agency customers.
Private vehicles, wheelchair-accessible arrangements, and dietary accommodations require separate confirmation. Prices and availability are not provided here.

## Price and availability limits

All amounts in these sample documents are fictional Indian rupee amounts.
Tax treatment is not specified. Ask the agency for a final tax-inclusive quote rather than assuming taxes are included or excluded.
No discounts or seasonal rates are documented.
If two documents disagree about a price, show both sources and ask the agency to resolve the discrepancy. Do not silently choose one.
