# Demonstration agency packages

These are fictional packages for testing this application. They are not real offers or current travel advice.

## Coimbatore discovery — DEMO

Our fictional Coimbatore discovery package lasts two days and one night. The demonstration price is INR 6,500 per person, based on two adults sharing a room.

The sample itinerary includes Marudhamalai Temple, the Gass Forest Museum, and a visit to the Isha Yoga Center. Visits are subject to opening schedules, which are not supplied in this document.

The package includes one night of accommodation, breakfast, and local transfers. It excludes flights, train tickets, entrance fees, lunch, and dinner. Hotel names and live availability are not provided.
